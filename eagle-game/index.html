Talon & Thermal

Gastineau Channel, near Juneau, Alaska

  • Steer with the arrow keys (or WASD). On a phone, drag anywhere.
  • Space flaps. Each press is one wingbeat; hold it to keep beating.
  • Hold Shift to fold your wings and fall. Let go and pull up to swoop out.
  • Hold C to flare and brake. Brake low and slow over land, or at the nest, to perch.
  • Circle inside the swirling thermals to climb without flapping.
  • Skim the water over a school to snatch a salmon, then carry it to the nest.
  • Fly straight through ravens at the nest. Hit a rival eagle at dive speed or it takes your fish.

No mouse needed: Enter starts, F starts free flight.
