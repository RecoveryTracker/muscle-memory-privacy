Talon & Thermal

Gastineau Channel, near Juneau, Alaska

  • Drag anywhere to steer (or WASD / arrow keys).
  • Hold Dive (Space) to fold your wings and fall. Let go and pull up to swoop out.
  • Circle inside the swirling thermals to climb. Flap (Shift) for a push.
  • Skim the water over a school to snatch a salmon, then carry it to the nest.
  • Fly straight through ravens raiding the nest. A rival eagle will come for your catch: hit it at dive speed or lose the fish.
