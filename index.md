# Privacy Policy for Muscle Memory

*Last updated: November 5, 2026*

## Introduction
Muscle Memory is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how the app handles your data on iPhone, iPad and Android.

## Information Collection
Muscle Memory does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking, and we run no server that receives your data.

## Data Storage
Everything you enter is stored locally on your device:
- Workout records
- Exercise lists
- Personal preferences
- Muscle group tracking information

## Data Usage
Your data is used solely within the app to:
- Track your workouts
- Monitor muscle group recovery
- Display workout history and progress
- Manage exercise routines

## Network Use
- **Android:** the app makes no network connections.
- **iPhone and iPad:** the app makes one lookup to Apple's App Store to check whether a newer version has been published, so it can tell you an update is available. It sends no personal information.
- **Optional iCloud backup (iPhone and iPad only):** if you turn it on, the app saves a backup of your workout data to **your own iCloud account**. It is off unless you enable it, the backup is held by Apple under your Apple ID, and we never have access to it.

## Data Protection
- Your data stays on your device unless you choose to back it up or export it
- The app relies on your device's built-in security
- No third-party analytics or tracking

## Your Choices
You can:
- Export a full backup of your data, or your workout history as a CSV file, from within the app
- Import a backup you previously exported
- Delete all app data by deleting the app or through your device settings

## Updates to Privacy Policy
We will notify users of any changes to this privacy policy through app updates.
